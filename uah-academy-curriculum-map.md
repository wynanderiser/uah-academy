# UAH Academy — Full Curriculum Map

*A working reference, not a live-synced document — update this by hand as more lessons get built.*

---

## Beginner Tier — ✓ Complete (6 of 6 built & tested)

No colour theory, no composition theory beyond focal point, no personal style. One fix at a time. Honesty over flattery, always.

1. **One Object, One Light** — basic proportion, value/light creating form, one focal point
2. **Two Objects, One Light** — relative proportion between two objects, consistent light across both
3. **Line & Edges** — line confidence, hard vs soft edges depending on light
4. **Simple Still Life** — 3–4 objects, one clear "star" with genuine composition
5. **Foreground & Background** — depth through contrast and detail restraint
6. **First Finished Piece** — synthesis, student's own subject, no new technique. Completing this triggers "Beginner Tier Complete" and the free greetings card reward.

---

## Intermediate Tier — in progress (1 of 6 built)

This is the tier where composition theory, colour theory, and perspective genuinely open up for the first time. Still exactly one fix at a time, still the same honesty rule — those turned out to be about good teaching, not beginner hand-holding.

1. **Perspective Basics** — one vanishing point, one horizon line, the core mechanic *(built)*
2. **Perspective Basics, Part 2: Horizon Placement** — where you put the horizon as a deliberate compositional choice, not a default *(building now)*
3. **Life vs Reference** — drawing from direct observation vs a flattened photo
4. **Composition in Depth** — rule of thirds, leading lines, negative space, framing
5. **Colour Basics** — colour theory and harmony (the Colour Companion tool becomes directly relevant here)
6. **Short Themed Series** — three pieces around one theme, a structured mini-portfolio

---

## Advanced Tier — planned, not yet built
