Beginner tier · Lesson 6: First Finished Piece

Show me a finished piece — entirely your choice.

Before you begin

Five lessons ago you were asked to look closely at one object and simply get its shape and light roughly honest. Since then you've compared two things against each other, learned that a line can mean different things depending on how hard it presses, arranged several objects so one of them actually mattered, and pushed a whole background quietly out of the way so a subject could come forward. None of that was really about the egg, or the mug, or the shadow — it was about learning to look properly, one habit at a time. This lesson doesn't teach anything new. It simply asks you to choose something that matters to you, and use everything you've already built to draw it as well as you honestly can.

Choose your own subject, and bring together everything from proportion to edges to composition in one considered piece. That's it — no new technique, just everything you already have, used well.

What you'll need
  • A subject of your own choosing — simple is still better than complicated; one to three objects, with a real background behind them
  • The same soft pencil (2B) that's served you throughout — or charcoal, from Lesson 3, if you're ready for it
  • The same single light source from one side
The exercise (45–90 minutes, or longer if you're enjoying it)
  1. Choose on purpose. Pick something that actually interests you — you're far more likely to look closely at something you care about.
  2. Work through what you know. Shape first, then light and shadow, then edges, then check the whole composition — the same order you've been practising all along, just applied together.
  3. Finish it properly. Take your time on this one. This is the piece the whole Beginner tier has been building toward — treat it like it matters, because it does.

That's the whole brief. This is the first piece you might genuinely want to keep.

Submitting it

Photograph it in natural daylight if you can, straight-on rather than at an angle, on a plain surface. Our AI teacher will provide feedback and offer suggestions. You can ask for more detail for a deeper analysis, and ask questions to clear up anything that's still confusing. This one marks the end of Beginner tier.
